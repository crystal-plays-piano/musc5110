The use of "non-tonality" - that is, an extremely intentional blend of tonal and atonal techniques and structures - and popular idioms - including Jazz, Blues, and various American Folk Musics - in Frederic Rzewski's *The People United Will Never Be Defeated* establishes a dual\* discourse of representations of class and race in the standard "Classical" piano repertoire that urges the pianist to dismantle imagined genre/stylistic barriers - "conventions" - and pursue a more holistic approach to both the piano instrument and a broader engagement with music as a whole.
